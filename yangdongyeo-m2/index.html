<보기>에서 어법상 옳은 문장만을 있는 대로 고른 것을 고르시오.

(a) I saw him crossing the street.

(b) She used to lived in Seoul.

(c) The book which I read it last week was fun.

(d) It was so cold that we stayed inside.

(e) We heard the baby cry at night.

  1. ①(a), (d)
  2. ②(a), (d), (e)
  3. ③(b), (c)
  4. ④(a), (c), (e)
  5. ⑤(b), (d), (e)
정답②

(b)는 used to live, (c)는 관계절 끝의 it 을 지웁니다.
