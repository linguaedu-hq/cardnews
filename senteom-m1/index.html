함정

점수가 깎이는 곳 9가지

  1. 1감각동사 뒤에 -ly 부사를 씀

    예

    This soup smells sweetly.

    This soup smells sweet.

  2. 2bad 의 비교급을 more bad 로 씀

    예

    My score is more bad than yours.

    My score is worse than yours.

  3. 3more 와 -er 을 함께 씀

    예

    My room is more wider than yours.

    My room is wider than yours.

함정

점수가 깎이는 곳 9가지

  1. 4buy · make 를 3형식으로 바꿀 때 to 를 씀

    예

    Dad bought a cap to me.

    Dad bought a cap for me.

  2. 54형식(사람 + 물건)에 전치사를 넣음

    예

    He showed to us his room.

    He showed us his room.

  3. 6동명사 주어 뒤에 are 를 씀

    예

    Reading comic books are fun.

    Reading comic books is fun.

함정

점수가 깎이는 곳 9가지

  1. 7enjoy 뒤에 to부정사를 씀

    예

    We enjoy playing chess.

  2. 8be동사 뒤 -ing 를 모두 진행형으로 봄
