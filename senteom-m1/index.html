함정

점수가 깎이는 곳 9가지

  1. 1감각동사 뒤에 -ly 부사를 씀

    예

    This soup smells sweetly.

    This soup smells sweet.

  2. 2bad 의 비교급을 more bad 로 씀

    예

    My score is more bad than yours.

    My score is worse than yours.

  3. 3more 와 -er 을 함께 씀

    예

    My room is more wider than yours.

    My room is wider than yours.

함정

점수가 깎이는 곳 9가지

  1. 4buy · make 를 3형식으로 바꿀 때 to 를 씀

    예

    Dad bought a cap to me.

    Dad bought a cap for me.

  2. 54형식(사람 + 물건)에 전치사를 넣음

    예

    He showed to us his room.

    He showed us his room.

  3. 6동명사 주어 뒤에 are 를 씀

    예

    Reading comic books are fun.

    Reading comic books is fun.

함정

점수가 깎이는 곳 9가지

  1. 7enjoy 뒤에 to부정사를 씀

    예

    We enjoy to play chess.

    We enjoy playing chess.

  2. 8be동사 뒤 -ing 를 모두 진행형으로 봄

    예

    동명사(굽는 것)My hobby is baking.

    현재분사(굽고 있다)Dad is baking now.

  3. 9우리말이 과거인데 현재형으로 씀

    예「신맛이 났다」

    The pie tastes sour.

    The pie tasted sour.

문제 3

마지막 체크 8문제

어법상 문장의 전환이 옳은 것은?

  1. ①She sent me a postcard. → She sent a postcard for me.
  2. ②Dad cooked us pasta. → Dad cooked pasta to us.
  3. ③Mr. Kim teaches us science. → Mr. Kim teaches science to us.
  4. ④I bought my sister a doll. → I bought a doll to my sister.
  5. ⑤He lent me his umbrella. → He lent his umbrella of me.
정답③

send · lend 는 to, cook · buy 는 for 를 씁니다.
