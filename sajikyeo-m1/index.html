함정

점수가 깎이는 곳 9가지

  1. 1감각동사 뒤에 -ly 부사를 씀

    예

    This soup smells deliciously.

    This soup smells delicious.

  2. 2-ing 를 쓰는 동사 뒤에 to부정사를 씀

    예enjoy · finish · keep · give up

    Mina finished to clean her room.

    Mina finished cleaning her room.

  3. 3to부정사를 쓰는 동사 뒤에 -ing 를 씀

    예want · hope · plan · decide · agree

    We decided visiting the museum.

    We decided to visit the museum.

함정

점수가 깎이는 곳 9가지

  1. 4동명사 주어 뒤에 복수 동사를 씀

    예

    Reading comic books are fun.

    Reading comic books is fun.

  2. 5it 으로 답할 때 시제를 맞추지 않음

    예What date will it be tomorrow?

    It is May 3rd.

    It will be May 3rd.

  3. 6형용사 앞에 don't · doesn't 를 씀

    예

    My brother doesn't busy today.

    My brother isn't busy today.

함정

점수가 깎이는 곳 9가지

  1. 7Does · Can 뒤에 동사에 -s 를 붙임

    예

  2. 8There 뒤 셀 수 없는 명사에 are 를 씀

    예
