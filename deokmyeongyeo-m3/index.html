5과 · 문법 ①

부정대명사 one(ones)

one = a(n) + 단수명사 (정해지지 않은 하나)

I lost my umbrella. I need to buy one.

ones = 복수명사

These cups are dirty. Do you have clean ones?

it = 앞에 나온 바로 그것

I lost my umbrella. I have to find it.

본문의 one = a maze

Here is one as an example.본문

함정

점수가 깎이는 곳 6가지

  1. 1능동인데 과거분사로 시작한 분사구문

    예

    Walked home, I met my teacher.

    Walking home, I met my teacher.

  2. 2분사구문의 not 을 분사 뒤에 씀

    예

    Feeling not well, she left early.

    Not feeling well, she left early.

  3. 3복수 명사를 one 으로 받음

    예

    My socks are old. I need new one.

    My socks are old. I need new ones.

함정

점수가 깎이는 곳 6가지

  1. 4강조 구문에 what 을 씀

    예

    It was Mina what called me.

    It was Mina that called me.

  2. 5강조 구문 It is 의 시제를 맞추지 않음

    예「내가 가방을 잃어버린 것은 바로 어제였다」

    It is yesterday that I lost my bag.

    It was yesterday that I lost my bag.

  3. 6however 로 두 문장을 쉼표만으로 이음

    예

    The room was small, however it was clean.

    The room was small. However, it was clean.

밑줄 친 부분을 분사구문으로 바꿔 문장을 다시 쓰시오.

(1) When he opened the box, he found an old letter.

→ .

(2) Because I don't have a bike, I walk to school.

→ .

조건
  • 접속사와 주어를 쓰지 말 것
  • 주어, 동사를 갖춘 완전한 문장으로 쓸 것
정답(1) Opening the box, he found an old letter.(2) Not having a bike, I walk to school.

접속사·주어를 지우고 -ing, 부정은 Not 을 분사 앞에 씁니다.

문제 2

마지막 체크 8문제

어법상 어색한 문장을 고르시오.

  1. ①Listening to music, she cleaned her room.
  2. ②Not knowing what to say, I kept silent.
  3. ③Seeing the police, the thief ran away.
  4. ④Smiled brightly, the baby looked at me.
  5. ⑤Turning left, you will see the bakery.

문제 3

마지막 체크 8문제

문제 5

마지막 체크 8문제

빈칸에 들어갈 말이 순서대로 짝지어진 것을 고르시오.

  • I need a pen. Can you lend me ______?
  • Where is my phone? I can't find ______.
  • The cups are dirty. Get clean ______.
  1. ①one – it – ones
  2. ②one – one – ones
  3. ③it – it – one

마지막 체크 8문제

Yuna found a wallet on the bus.

(1) Yuna 를 강조

→ .

(2) on the bus 를 강조

→ .

조건
