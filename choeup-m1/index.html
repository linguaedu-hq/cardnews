Lesson 5. Think like Sherlock Holmes!

Lesson 6. Good for the Earth, Good for Us

6과 · 대화

부탁하기 · 거절하기

부탁하기

Can you (please) + 동사원형 ~?

Can you please fix my bike?

Sure. No problem.

거절하기

I'm sorry, but I can't. + 이유

I'm busy now.

6과 · 문법 ①

to부정사 명사적 용법

목적어: want · plan · decide + to + 동사원형

I decided to fix it and reuse it.본문

주어: To + 동사원형 ~ + is

To walk every day is good for you.

보어: be동사 + to + 동사원형

My dream is to be a vet.

부정: not + to + 동사원형

I decided not to go.

함정 3개
  • want to buying → to buy
  • To ~ 주어 뒤 are → is
  • enjoy · mind 뒤에는 -ing
6과 · 문법 ②

접속사 that

think · hear + that + 주어 + 동사

접속사 that 은 생략할 수 있음

I guess it's all right.본문

지시어 that = 저것 · 그것 (생략 )

함정

점수가 깎이는 곳 8가지

  1. 1비교급에 more 와 -er 를 함께 씀

    예

    My dog is more bigger than yours.

    My dog is bigger than yours.

  2. 2최상급 뒤 of 와 in 을 바꿔 씀

    예of + 여럿 · in + 장소

    Jeju is the largest island of Korea.

    Jeju is the largest island in Korea.

함정

점수가 깎이는 곳 8가지

  1. 3There is / are 를 뒤 명사의 수에 맞추지 않음

    예

    There is three eggs in the basket.

    There are three eggs in the basket.

  2. 4must not 뒤에 to 를 넣음

    예

    You must not to park here.

    You must not park here.

  3. 5want · decide + to 뒤에 동사원형을 안 씀

    예

    She decided to joins the club.

    She decided to join the club.

함정

점수가 깎이는 곳 8가지

  1. 6to부정사 주어 뒤에 are 를 씀

    예

    To read books are fun.

    To read books is fun.

  2. 7접속사 that 뒤를 의문문 순서로 씀

    예

    I think that is the movie sad.

    I think that the movie is sad.

  3. 8「단어를 전부 활용」 조건에서 the 를 빠뜨림

    예<보기>에 the 가 있음

    Minho is tallest in his class.

    Minho is the tallest in his class.
