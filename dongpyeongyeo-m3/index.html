함정

점수가 깎이는 곳 8가지

  1. 1먼저 일어난 일에 현재완료를 씀

    예

    When I arrived, the class has already begun.

    When I arrived, the class had already begun.

  2. 2had 뒤에 과거형을 씀

    예

    He had wrote a letter by noon.

    He had written a letter by noon.

  3. 3강조 구문에 what 을 씀

    예

    It was Jina what won the prize.

    It was Jina that won the prize.

함정

점수가 깎이는 곳 8가지

  1. 4지난 일을 강조하면서 It is 로 씀

    예「내가 그를 본 것은 어제였다」

    It is yesterday that I saw him.

    It was yesterday that I saw him.

  2. 5분사구문의 not 을 분사 뒤에 씀

    예

    Knowing not his name, I couldn't call him.

    Not knowing his name, I couldn't call him.

  3. 6조동사 수동태에서 be 를 빠뜨림

    예

    Doors must locked at night.

    Doors must be locked at night.

함정

점수가 깎이는 곳 8가지

  1. 7가정법 과거의 주절에 will 을 씀

    예

    If I had wings, I will fly home.

    If I had wings, I would fly home.

  2. 8성격을 나타내는 형용사 뒤에 for 를 씀

    예kind · wise · nice 뒤는 of

    It was wise for her to wait.

    It was wise of her to wait.

문제 1

마지막 체크 8문제

어법상 옳은 문장을 고르시오.

  1. ①I found that I have left my phone at home.
  2. ②She said she had seen the movie before.
  3. ③The game has already begun when we reached the stadium.
  4. ④He had finish his report before class.
  5. ⑤They had went home before it rained.
정답②

말한 때(said)보다 먼저 본 일이라 had seen 이 옳습니다. ①은 had left, ③은 had already begun, ④는 had finished, ⑤는 had gone 으로 고칩니다.

→ .

(the letter, must, send, today)
