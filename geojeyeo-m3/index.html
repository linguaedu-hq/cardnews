8과 · 문법 ①

to부정사의 의미상 주어 for · of

hard · easy · impossible 뒤에는 for 목적격

It was impossible for him to catch any fish.본문

성격·태도 형용사(kind · nice · foolish) 뒤에는 of

It is nice of you to help me.교과서

함정 3개

    It was kind for you to help me.

    It was kind of you to help me.

    성격을 말하는 kind 는 of 를 씁니다.

  • for he → for him

    It is hard for I to get up early.

    It is hard for me to get up early.

    for 뒤에는 목적격 me 를 씁니다.

  • to goes → to go

    I want to learning Spanish.

    I want to learn Spanish.

    to 뒤에는 동사원형을 씁니다.

8과 · 문법 ②

가정법 과거

If + 과거형, would · could + 동사원형

작년 시험

함정 3개
  • If I have → had눌러서 펼쳐 보세요

    If I have a car, I could drive you home.

    If I had a car, I could drive you home.

    가정법 과거이므로 if절에 과거형 had 를 씁니다.

  • will → would

    Were I you, I will take the bus.

    Were I you, I would take the bus.

    가정법 주절에는 would 를 씁니다.

  • If I was → 시험 답은 were

    If it were sunny, we could go on a picnic.
