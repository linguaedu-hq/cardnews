  1. ①Tom is as taller as his father.
  2. ②She sings as good as a singer.
  3. ③This box is as heavy as that one.
  4. ④He is not as older as he looks.
  5. ⑤I can run as fastest as you.
정답③

① tall ② well ④ old ⑤ fast 로 고칩니다.
