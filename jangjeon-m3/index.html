    예작년 서답형 5 조건 — 반의어 활용 · not 사용 시 감점

    It is not usual to see a rainbow.

    It is unusual to see a rainbow.

ㄴ. As I didn't know the answer, I kept silent.

  4. ④ㄱ, ㄷ, ㄹ
