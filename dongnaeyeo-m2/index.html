6과 · 문법 ①

so ~ that — 너무 ~해서 …하다

so + 형용사·부사 + that + 주어 + 동사

He studied so hard that he was able …본문

과거면 that 뒤 couldn't · could

I was so excited that I couldn't sleep all night.

that 절바꿔 쓰기예
can't · couldn'ttoo ~ totoo hot to eat
can · could~ enough totall enough to reach
  • 2022 2학기 기말 서답형3 · 12점 so ~ that 4문장 영작
  • 2023 2학기 기말 서답형5 · 8점 단어 2개씩 골라 영작 4개
함정 2개
  • too hot to eat it → it 지움눌러서 펼쳐 보세요

    The soup was too hot for me to eat it.

    The soup was too hot for me to eat.

    it 은 문장 주어 the soup 을 가리키므로 지웁니다.

  • so that + 주어 + can = ~하도록

    Tom studied hard so that he could pass the test.

    '~하도록·~하기 위해'로 해석되면 목적 so that, '너무 ~해서 …하다'로 해석되면 결과 so ~ that 입니다.

6과 · 문법 ②

지각동사 + 목적어 + -ing

see · hear + 목적어 + -ing

… heard people shouting …본문

목적어 뒤 동사원형도 맞음

목적어 뒤예
-ing · 동사원형 saw him running
to부정사 saw him to run
과거형 · -s saw him ran

  • 2023 2학기 중간 서답형2 · 12점 그림 영작
함정 2개
  • heard they singing → them눌러서 펼쳐 보세요

    We heard they singing in the next room.

    We heard them singing in the next room.

    목적어는 목적격 them 으로 씁니다.

  • watched him plays → play

    I watched him plays the drums.

    I watched him play the drums.

    목적어 뒤 동사에 -s 를 붙이지 않습니다.

함정

점수가 깎이는 곳 8가지

  1. 1「that 은 사용하지 말 것」 조건에 that 을 씀

    예조건: 관계대명사 that 금지

    The book that I read was fun.

    The book which I read was fun.

  2. 2관계절 끝에 목적어를 또 씀

    예

    The photo which Dad took it is old.

    The photo which Dad took is old.

함정

점수가 깎이는 곳 8가지

  1. 3사람 선행사에 which 를 씀

    예

    The actor which we met at the festival was kind.

    The actor whom we met at the festival was kind.

  2. 4used to 뒤에 -ing 를 씀

    예

    My aunt used to teaching math.

    My aunt used to teach math.

  3. 5be used to(익숙하다) 뒤에 동사원형을 씀

    예

    Dad is used to walk to work.

    Dad is used to walking to work.

함정

점수가 깎이는 곳 8가지

  1. 6지각동사 + 목적어 뒤에 to부정사를 씀

    예

    I saw the bus to leave.

    I saw the bus leave.

  2. 7too ~ to 문장 끝에 it 을 남김

    예

    This soup is too salty to eat it.

    This soup is too salty to eat.

  3. 8「완전한 문장」 조건에 낱말만 씀

    예질문: What kinds of fruit do you like?

    Apples and grapes.

    I like apples and grapes.
