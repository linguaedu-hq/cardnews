함정

점수가 깎이는 곳 8가지

  1. 1tell·ask + 목적어 뒤에 to 를 빠뜨림

    예

    Mom told me practice daily.

    Mom told me to practice daily.

  2. 2'~하지 말라고'를 don't 로 씀

    예

    The nurse told us don't touch it.

    The nurse told us not to touch it.

  3. 3사람 선행사에 which 를 씀

    예

    The singer which I like is Jay.

    The singer who(m) I like is Jay.

함정

점수가 깎이는 곳 8가지

  1. 4관계절 끝에 목적어를 또 씀

    예

    The box which I made it is big.

    The box which I made is big.

  2. 5지각동사 + 목적어 뒤에 to부정사

    예

    I saw Tom to dance.

    I saw Tom dance(dancing).

  3. 6간접의문문에서 동사를 주어 앞에 둠

    예

    I know where is the bank.

    I know where the bank is.

함정

점수가 깎이는 곳 8가지

  1. 7간접의문문에 does·did 를 남김

    예

    Tell me when does it start.

    Tell me when it starts.

  2. 8느낀 사람에게 -ing 감정 분사

    예

    We were exciting about the trip.

    We were excited about the trip.

문제 3

마지막 체크 8문제

두 문장을 한 문장으로 바꿔 쓰시오.

I lost the umbrella.

My aunt gave it to me.

→ .

조건
  • 관계대명사 which 를 쓸 것
  • 관계절 안에 it 을 남기지 말 것
정답I lost the umbrella which my aunt gave to me.

사물 선행사에 which 를 쓰고 gave 뒤의 it 을 지웁니다(to me 는 남깁니다).
