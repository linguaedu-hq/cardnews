감정 표현하기 · 축하·유감 말하기

축하하기

Congratulations (on ~)!

I'm so excited.

That's wonderful. Congratulations!

유감 말하기

I lost my smartphone. I'm upset.

함정 2개

  • 내 감정은 excited (exciting )
6과 · 문법 ①

과거완료 had + 과거분사

기준 과거보다 먼저 일어난 일

수동: had been + 과거분사

작년 시험
  • 12~16번 지문 had got · had been cut off 가 나옴
  • 예상 서답형 조건 영작 — 「과거완료를 활용할 것」
함정 3개
  • had went → had gone눌러서 펼쳐 보세요

    She lost the pen that I had gave her.

    She lost the pen that I had given her.

    had 뒤에는 과거분사 given 을 씁니다.

  • 기준이 과거 — has started → had started

    When we arrived, the movie has already started.

    When we arrived, the movie had already started.

    기준이 과거(arrived)이므로 had 를 씁니다.

  • 의문사 + 주어 + had: where he had got

    Can you tell me where is the station?

    Can you tell me where the station is?

    문장 안에서는 주어 + 동사 순서로 씁니다.

6과 · 문법 ②

관계대명사 what

what = the thing(s) that 「~하는 것」

The table was what every dealer dreamed of.본문

what절이 주어 → 단수 동사

What you need is a good long holiday.

작년 시험에 나온 문장

작년 시험
  • 8번 · 5점 어법상 옳지 않은 문장의 개수 — what 문장 5개
함정 3개
  • anything what you want → that눌러서 펼쳐 보세요

    The thing what I need now is some sleep.

    the thing that I need

    선행사 the thing 이 있으므로 that 을 씁니다.

  • what + 빠진 말 없는 문장 → that

    I believe what she is honest.

    I believe that she is honest.

    what 뒤가 빠진 것이 없는 문장이므로 접속사 that 을 씁니다.

  • 선행사가 없으면 that → what

    What I need now is some sleep.

함정

점수가 깎이는 곳 9가지

  1. 1문장에 동사가 있는데 명사 뒤에 동사를 또 씀

    예

    The boy plays the drums is Tom.

    The boy playing the drums is Tom.

  2. 2「~된」 뜻인데 현재분사를 씀

    예

    I read a letter writing in English.

    I read a letter written in English.

  3. 3as 와 as 사이에 비교급을 씀

    예

    This bag is as heavier as that one.

    This bag is as heavy as that one.

함정

점수가 깎이는 곳 9가지

  1. 4동사를 꾸미는데 as ~ as 사이에 형용사를 씀

    예

    Mina sings as beautiful as Suji.

    Mina sings as beautifully as Suji.

  2. 5선행사 뒤에 what 을 씀

    예

    This is the cap what I wanted.

    This is the cap that I wanted.

  3. 6what 뒤에 빠진 말이 없는 절을 씀

    예

    I know what he ordered a pizza.

    I know what he ordered.

함정

점수가 깎이는 곳 9가지

  1. 7과거보다 먼저 일어난 일에 has 를 씀

    예

    When I woke up, the bus has left.

    When I woke up, the bus had left.

  2. 8동명사 주어 뒤에 복수 동사를 씀

    예

    Washing the dishes are my job.

    Washing the dishes is my job.

  3. 9셀 수 있는 명사 앞에 a little 을 씀

    예

    There are a little apples in the box.

    There are a few apples in the box.
