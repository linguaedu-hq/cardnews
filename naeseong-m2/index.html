함정

점수가 깎이는 곳 8가지

  1. 1관계절 끝에 목적어를 또 씀

    예

    The pie which I made it was hot.

    The pie which I made was hot.

  2. 2주격 관계대명사를 생략함

    예

    The girl sings well is Amy.

    The girl who sings well is Amy.

  3. 3「관계대명사를 생략하지 말 것」 조건에 생략함

    예「관계대명사를 생략하지 않고 사용할 것」

    The song I like is sad.

    The song which I like is sad.

함정

점수가 깎이는 곳 8가지

  1. 4used to 뒤에 -ing

    예

    I used to jogging every day.

    I used to jog every day.

  2. 5지각동사 목적어 뒤에 to부정사

    예

    I saw my brother to wash the car.

    I saw my brother washing the car.

  3. 6지각동사 목적어를 주격으로 씀

    예

    We heard they laughing outside.

    We heard them laughing outside.

함정

점수가 깎이는 곳 8가지

  1. 7so ~ that 뒤 시제를 현재로 씀

    예

    The room was so hot that I open the window.

    The room was so hot that I opened the window.

  2. 8too ~ to 문장 끝에 it 을 남김

    예

    The box was too big to move it.

    The box was too big to move.
