함정

점수가 깎이는 곳 8가지

  1. 1분사구가 붙은 주어 뒤에서 동사의 수를 틀림

    예

    The boys playing soccer is tired.

    The boys playing soccer are tired.

  2. 2동작을 당하는 명사를 현재분사로 꾸밈

    예

    I read a letter writing in English.

    I read a letter written in English.

  3. 3as ~ as 부정형으로 바꿀 때 두 대상의 순서를 그대로 둠

    예Jin is taller than Leo.

    Jin is not as tall as Leo.

    Leo is not as tall as Jin.

함정

점수가 깎이는 곳 8가지

  1. 4과거보다 먼저 일어난 일에 has 를 씀

    예

    When I got to the station, the train had already left.

  2. 5간접의문문을 의문문 순서로 씀

    예

    She asked me where I had been.

  3. 6선행사 뒤에 what 을 씀

    예

    This is the bag what I bought.

    This is the bag that I bought.
