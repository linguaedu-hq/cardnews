  • If I have → had눌러서 펼쳐 보세요

    If I have a car, I could drive you home.

    If I had a car, I could drive you home.

    가정법 과거이므로 if절에 과거형 had 를 씁니다.

  • will → would

    Were I you, I will take the bus.

    Were I you, I would take the bus.

    가정법 주절에는 would 를 씁니다.

  • 긍정 ↔ 부정을 반대로

    If I had time, I could help you.

    As I don't have time, I can't help you.
