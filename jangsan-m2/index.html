  2. 2기간 앞에 since 를 씀

    예

    I have been here since an hour.

    I have been here for an hour.

  3. 3tell · ask + 목적어 뒤에 to 를 빠뜨림

    예

    The coach told us run faster.

    The coach told us to run faster.

함정

점수가 깎이는 곳 8가지

  1. 4의미상 주어를 주격으로 씀

    예

    It is hard for he to get up early.

    It is hard for him to get up early.

  2. 5The + 비교급에 원급이나 more -er 를 씀

    예

    The more bigger, the better.

    The bigger, the better.

  3. 6뒤 절의 the + 비교급을 앞에 두지 않음

    예

    The more I eat, I am the happier.

    The more I eat, the happier I am.

함정

점수가 깎이는 곳 8가지

  1. 7each · every 뒤에 복수 동사를 씀

    예

    Every room have a window.

    Every room has a window.

  2. 8지각동사 목적어 뒤에 to부정사를 씀

    예

    We heard the dog to bark.

    We heard the dog barking.
