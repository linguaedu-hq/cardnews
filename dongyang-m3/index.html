6과 · 문법 ①

과거완료 had + 과거분사

기준 과거보다 먼저 일어난 일

When I came home, everybody had gone to bed.교과서

that절 안 — 주절보다 먼저 일어난 일

He told me that he had already seen the movie.교과서

수동: had been + 과거분사

The legs of the table had been cut off.교과서

  • 2022 1학기 기말 서답형 8 · 8점 시제 조건 영작 · 각 4점
함정 3개
  • had went → had gone
  • 기준이 과거 — has left → had left
  • yesterday 만 있으면 과거시제
6과 · 문법 ②

관계대명사 what

what = the thing(s) that 「~하는 것」

We ate what Mom had cooked for us.교과서

what절이 주어 → 동사는 단수

What he wants is only the legs.본문

  • 2022 1학기 기말 서답형 7 · 6점 관계대명사로 두 문장 잇기 · 각 3점
  • 2022 1학기 기말 10번 · 4점 쓰임이 다른 것
함정 3개
  • the thing what → that · which
  • 빠진 말 없는 문장 앞 what → that
  • 의문사 what(무엇)과 구별

함정

점수가 깎이는 곳 8가지

  1. 1as 와 as 사이에 비교급을 씀

    예

    This bag is as heavier as mine.

    This bag is as heavy as mine.

  2. 2동사를 꾸미는데 as 사이에 형용사를 씀

    예

    Mina sings as beautiful as you.

    Mina sings as beautifully as you.

함정

점수가 깎이는 곳 8가지

  1. 3명사가 직접 하는 동작인데 과거분사로 꾸밈

    예

    The girl talked to Tom is my sister.

    The girl talking to Tom is my sister.

  2. 4분사구문의 not 을 분사 뒤에 씀

    예

    Feeling not well, he went home.

    Not feeling well, he went home.

  3. 5had 뒤에 과거형을 씀

    예

    The concert had already began when we arrived.

    The concert had already begun when we arrived.

함정

점수가 깎이는 곳 8가지

  1. 6선행사 뒤에 what 을 씀

    예

    I lost the pen what Dad gave me.

    I lost the pen that Dad gave me.

  2. 7접속사 as 와 so 를 함께 씀

    예

    As it was late, so we went home.

    As it was late, we went home.

  3. 8suggest 뒤에 to부정사를 씀

    예

    I suggest you to drink more water.

    I suggest you drink more water.
