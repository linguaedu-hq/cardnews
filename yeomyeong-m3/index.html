함정

점수가 깎이는 곳 9가지

  1. 1분사구문을 과거분사로 시작함

    예내가 한 일(능동)은 -ing

    Heard the news, she cried.

    Hearing the news, she cried.

  2. 2분사구문 부정에서 not 위치가 틀림

    예

    Feeling not well, I went home.

    Not feeling well, I went home.

  3. 3not only A but also B 에서 A · B 형태가 다름

    예

    She likes not only reading but also to go hiking.

    She likes not only reading but also going hiking.

함정

점수가 깎이는 곳 9가지

  1. 4목적어가 빠진 절 앞에 where 를 씀

    예visited 뒤 목적어가 빠지면 which

    That is the zoo where we visited.

    That is the zoo which we visited.

  2. 5the way 와 how 를 함께 씀

    예

    This is the way how he did it.

    This is how he did it.

  3. 6however 로 두 문장을 이음

    예

    It was cold, however we went out.

    It was cold. However, we went out.

점수가 깎이는 곳 9가지

  1. 7whose 뒤에 소유격을 또 씀

    예

  2. 8가정법 과거 주절에 will 을 씀
