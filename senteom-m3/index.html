점수가 깎이는 곳 9가지

  1. 1가정법 과거의 if 절에 would 를 씀

    예「날개가 있다면 날아갈 텐데」

    If I would have wings, I would fly.

    If I had wings, I would fly.

  2. 2whose 를 쓸 곳에 who · which 를 씀

    예「친구의 아빠」처럼 소유이면 whose

    I have a friend who dad is a pilot.

    I have a friend whose dad is a pilot.

  3. 3The more 와 명사를 떼어 씀

    예

    The more you make friends, the happier you feel.

    The more friends you make, the happier you feel.

함정

점수가 깎이는 곳 9가지

  1. 4강조할 말을 It was 와 that 사이에 넣지 않음

    예

    It was that my dad baked this cake.

    It was my dad that baked this cake.

  2. 5prefer 뒤에 than 을 씀

    예

    I prefer tea than coffee.

    I prefer tea to coffee.

  3. 6간접화법에서 인칭 · 시제를 바꾸지 않음

    예She said, "I am tired."

    She said that I am tired.

    She said that she was tired.

함정

점수가 깎이는 곳 9가지

  1. 7if(~인지) 뒤를 의문문 어순으로 씀

    예

    I wonder if the news is true.

  2. 8둘 가운데 나머지 하나에 another 를 씀

  3. 9tell · ask 의 목적어 뒤에 동사원형을 씀

    My mom told me to clean my room.

마지막 체크 8문제

우리말을 <예시>를 참고하여 <조건>에 맞게 영작하시오.

< 예 시 >

If I had a tent, I would sleep outside.

(1) 나에게 시간이 더 있다면, 나는 그 책을 읽을 텐데. ( if, more time, the book )

→ .

(2) 그가 여기에 있다면, 우리는 함께 축구를 할 수 있을 텐데. ( if, here, together )

→ .

조건
  • <예시>와 같은 문장 형식으로 쓸 것
  • 주어진 단어를 반드시 포함할 것
정답(1) If I had more time, I would read the book.(2) If he were here, we could play soccer together.

if 절은 과거형, 주절은 would · could

문제 3

마지막 체크 8문제

다음 글을 읽고 주어진 문장을 <조건>에 맞게 영작하시오.

Mina has a neighbor. His dog barks every night. Mina cannot sleep well because of the noise.

Mina 에게는 개가 매일 밤 짖는 이웃이 있습니다.

→ .

조건
  • who / whom / whose 중 하나를 사용할 것
  • 윗글에 나와 있는 표현들만 사용할 것
정답Mina has a neighbor whose dog barks every night.

His 를 whose 로 바꿔 잇습니다.

문제 4

마지막 체크 8문제

어법상 어색한 것은?

  1. ①The harder you study, the better grades you will get.
  2. ②The more we walk, the healthier we get.
  3. ③The older he grew, the wiser he became.
  4. ④The more carefully you listen, you understand more.
  5. ⑤The earlier we go, the sooner we arrive.
정답④

뒤 절도 the + 비교급으로 시작합니다.

문제 5

마지막 체크 8문제

우리말에 맞게 <조건>에 맞춰 영작하시오.

우리가 더 많은 나무를 심을수록, 공기는 더 깨끗해진다. ( plant, clean, become )

→ .

조건
  • 「The + 비교급 ~, the + 비교급 ~」 구문으로 쓸 것
  • 주어진 단어를 모두 사용하되 필요시 형태를 바꿀 것
정답The more trees we plant, the cleaner the air becomes.

more trees 를 묶고 cleaner 로 씁니다.

문제 6

마지막 체크 8문제

밑줄 친 문장을 주어를 강조하는 문장으로 <조건>에 맞게 바꿔 쓰시오.

I left my bag in a taxi last night. I looked for it everywhere, but I couldn't find it. The driver found my bag. He called me this morning.

→ .

조건
  • ‘It was … that ~’ 강조구문을 사용할 것
  • 밑줄 친 문장에 있는 단어는 모두 사용할 것
정답It was the driver that found my bag.

주어를 It was 와 that 사이에 둡니다.

문제 7

마지막 체크 8문제

다음 문장을 간접화법으로 바르게 바꾼 것은?

Jim said to me, "I am using your pen."

  1. ①Jim told me that I am using your pen.
  2. ②Jim told me that he is using my pen.
  3. ③Jim told me that he was using my pen.
  4. ④Jim told me that he was using your pen.
  5. ⑤Jim said me that he was using my pen.
정답③

I → he, your → my, am → was

마지막 체크 8문제

• I have two sisters. One is a nurse, and (A) is a teacher.

• My coach told me (B) harder.

| (A) | (B)

  1. ①another | practice
  2. ②another | to practice
  3. ③the other | practice
  4. ④the other | to practice
  5. ⑤others | practicing
정답④

the other · tell + 목적어 + to부정사
