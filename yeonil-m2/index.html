6과 · 문법 ①

수동태

be동사 + 과거분사 (+ by 행위자)

부정문: be동사 + not + 과거분사

때형태예
현재am · is · are + 과거분사is cleaned

함정 2개

  • It was happened → happened
6과 · 문법 ②

so ~ that

so + 형용사 · 부사 + that + 주어 + 동사

so ~ that + 주어 + couldn't = too ~ to

He was too tired to run.

형태뜻
so ~ that너무 ~해서 …하다(결과)
so that ~ can~하도록(목적)
too ~ to너무 ~해서 …할 수 없다
  • 기출 0회 지난 2회 시험에 없었습니다
함정 2개
  • such tired that → so tired
  • too hot to eat it → it 삭제

함정

점수가 깎이는 곳 8가지

  1. 1주격 관계대명사 뒤 동사의 수가 틀림

    예

    I have a friend who live in Jeju.

    I have a friend who lives in Jeju.

  2. 2관계절 안에 주어를 또 씀

    예

    The boy who he sits here is Jun.

    The boy who sits here is Jun.

  3. 3주격 관계대명사를 빼고 씀

    예

    The boy won the race is Tom.

    The boy who won the race is Tom.

함정

점수가 깎이는 곳 8가지

  1. 4지각동사 + 목적어 뒤에 to부정사를 씀

    예

    We saw a bird to fly over the lake.

    We saw a bird flying over the lake.

  2. 5수동태에서 be동사를 빠뜨림

    예

    The cup broken by Leo.

    The cup was broken by Leo.

  3. 6수동태 be동사를 주어의 수에 맞추지 않음

    예

    The cookies was made by Dad.

    The cookies were made by Dad.

함정

점수가 깎이는 곳 8가지

  1. 7so ~ that 의 that 뒤 시제를 맞추지 않음

    예

    I was so sleepy that I can't read.

    I was so sleepy that I couldn't read.

  2. 8「빈칸의 개수에 맞게」에서 칸 수를 세지 않음

    예빈칸 6개 · 「그는 그 질문에 답할 수 없었다」

    He couldn't answer the question.

    He could not answer the question.

밑줄 친 who 의 쓰임이 나머지 넷과 다른 것을 고르시오.

  1. ①I know a boy who plays chess well.
  2. ②She is the singer who won the prize.
  3. ③I wonder who left this umbrella here.
  4. ④He has a sister who studies art.
  5. ⑤The woman who lives upstairs is a nurse.
정답③

③만 의문사(누가), 나머지는 관계대명사입니다.

(big, need)

문제 8

마지막 체크 8문제

It was very hot, so I couldn't sleep.

  1. ①It was so hot that I couldn't sleep.
  2. ②It was so hot that I can't sleep.
  3. ③It was so hot that I could sleep.

  5. ⑤It was such hot that I couldn't sleep.
