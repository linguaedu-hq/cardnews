함정

점수가 깎이는 곳 9가지

  1. 1분사구문을 과거형으로 시작함

    예

    Felt sick, Mina went home.

    Feeling sick, Mina went home.

  2. 2분사구문의 not 을 분사 뒤에 씀

    예

    Knowing not the rules, I lost.

    Not knowing the rules, I lost.

  3. 3「부사절로」 조건인데 주어 + 동사를 쓰지 않음

    예Waiting for the bus, I read a book.

    While waiting for the bus, I read a book.

    While I was waiting for the bus, I read a book.

함정

점수가 깎이는 곳 9가지

  1. 4not only A but also B 의 A · B 형태가 다름

    예

    She not only sings well but also dancing well.

    She not only sings well but also dances well.

  2. 5장소 뒤에 「주어 + 동사」가 완전한데 which 를 씀

    예

    This is the park which I met Jake.

    This is the park where I met Jake.

  3. 6결과를 말하는 문장 앞에 However 를 씀

    예

    It rained hard. However, the game was canceled.

    It rained hard. Thus, the game was canceled.

함정

점수가 깎이는 곳 9가지

  1. 7가정법 과거의 be동사를 was 로 씀

    예

    If it was sunny, we would go out.

    If it were sunny, we would go out.

  2. 8가정법 과거의 주절에 will 을 씀

    예

    If I had a map, I will find the way.

    If I had a map, I would find the way.

  3. 9「~의」 뜻인데 who 를 씀

    예

    I met a boy who aunt is a singer.

    I met a boy whose aunt is a singer.
