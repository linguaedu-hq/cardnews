함정

점수가 깎이는 곳 9가지

  1. 1최상급 앞의 the 를 빠뜨림

    예

    Seoul is biggest city in Korea.

    Seoul is the biggest city in Korea.

  2. 2more 와 -er 을 함께 씀

    예

    Tom is more taller than Jin.

    Tom is taller than Jin.

  3. 3one of the + 최상급 뒤에 단수명사를 씀

    예

    He is one of the best player.

    He is one of the best players.

함정

점수가 깎이는 곳 9가지

  1. 4There 뒤 명사의 수를 보지 않고 be동사를 씀

    예

    There is two cats on the sofa.

    There are two cats on the sofa.

  2. 5There 의문문의 어순을 틀림

    예

    There are any eggs in the box?

    Are there any eggs in the box?

  3. 6to 뒤에 동사원형이 아닌 꼴을 씀

    예

    I plan to visits my uncle.

    I plan to visit my uncle.

함정

점수가 깎이는 곳 9가지

  1. 7지시어 that 과 접속사 that 을 혼동함

    예

    지시어(저 ~)I like that cap.

    접속사I think that it is nice.

  2. 8that 절 동사의 -s 를 빠뜨림

    예that 뒤 주어가 3인칭 단수

    I think that Mina like dogs.

    I think that Mina likes dogs.

  3. 9「완전한 문장」 조건에서 주어·동사를 뺌

    예

    Older than my sister.

    He is older than my sister.
