함정

점수가 깎이는 곳 9가지

  1. 1so ~ that 절에서 목적어를 빠뜨림

    예

    The bag was so heavy that I couldn't carry.

    The bag was so heavy that I couldn't carry it.

  2. 2지각동사의 목적어 뒤에 to부정사를 씀

    예

    I heard a dog to bark outside.

    I heard a dog bark outside.

  3. 3I wish 뒤에 현재형을 씀

    예

    I wish I can speak Chinese well.

    I wish I could speak Chinese well.

함정

점수가 깎이는 곳 9가지

  1. 4강조구문 that 뒤 동사를 강조한 말에 맞추지 않음

    예

    It is my sisters that wakes me up every morning.

    It is my sisters that wake me up every morning.

  2. 5분사구문의 not 을 분사 뒤에 씀

    예

    Knowing not the way, he asked a police officer.

    Not knowing the way, he asked a police officer.

  3. 6형용사 + to부정사 뒤에 목적어를 또 씀

    예

    This book is easy to read it.

    This book is easy to read.

함정

점수가 깎이는 곳 9가지

  1. 7So + 동사 + 주어에서 동사와 격을 틀림

    예

    Mina was late, and so did me.

    Mina was late, and so was I.

  2. 8whether 절을 의문문 어순으로 씀

    예

    I wonder whether is she home.

    I wonder whether she is home.

  3. 9It's time 뒤에 현재형을 씀

    예

    It's time we start the meeting.

    It's time we started the meeting.
