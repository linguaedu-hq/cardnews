함정

점수가 깎이는 곳 9가지

  1. 1가정법 과거의 if절에 현재형을 씀

    예

    If I am rich, I would buy a boat.

    If I were rich, I would buy a boat.

  2. 2가정법 과거의 주절에 will 을 씀

    예

    If I had a car, I will drive there.

    If I had a car, I would drive there.

  3. 3조건문과 가정법 과거를 구별하지 않음

    예

    일어날 수 있는 일(~한다면 ~할 거야)If it rains tomorrow, I will stay home.

    지금 사실과 반대(~라면 ~할 텐데)If it rained right now, I would stay home.

함정

점수가 깎이는 곳 9가지

  1. 4「의문사 + to부정사」 주어 뒤에 복수 동사를 씀

    예

    Where to sit are not important.

    Where to sit is not important.

  2. 5so that 뒤에 조동사 + 동사원형을 쓰지 않음

    예

    I ran so that I won the race.

    I ran so that I could win the race.

  3. 6in order to 의 부정에서 not 의 위치를 틀림

    예

    Be quiet in order to not wake him.

    Be quiet in order not to wake him.

함정

점수가 깎이는 곳 9가지

  1. 7enough 를 형용사 앞에 씀

    예

    The room is enough large to hold fifty people.

    The room is large enough to hold fifty people.

  2. 8동사를 꾸미는 곳에 형용사 + enough 를 씀

    예

    The team played good enough to win the final.

    The team played well enough to win the final.

  3. 9괄호 안 단어를 빼고 다른 표현으로 씀

    예「(so, to)를 반드시 활용할 것」

    I got up early to study.

    I got up early so as to study.
