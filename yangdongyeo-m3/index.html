함정

점수가 깎이는 곳 8가지

  1. 1분사구를 명사 앞에 둠

    예분사 뒤에 말이 붙으면 명사 뒤

    Look at the wearing a blue hat girl.

    Look at the girl wearing a blue hat.

  2. 2당하는 쪽인데 -ing 를 씀

    예

    I have a phone making in Korea.

    I have a phone made in Korea.

  3. 3as 와 as 사이에 비교급을 씀

    예

    My cat is as bigger as your dog.

    My cat is as big as your dog.

함정

점수가 깎이는 곳 8가지

  1. 4셀 수 있는 명사에 as much 를 씀

    예

    I have as much friends as Jiho.

    I have as many friends as Jiho.

  2. 5과거보다 먼저 일어난 일을 현재완료로 씀

    예기준 = 과거 동사 woke

    When I woke up, Dad has left.

    When I woke up, Dad had left.

  3. 6had 뒤에 과거형을 씀

    예

    He said he had ate lunch.

    He said he had eaten lunch.

함정

점수가 깎이는 곳 8가지

  1. 7선행사 뒤에 what 을 씀

    예

    This is the bag what I bought.

    This is the bag that I bought.

  2. 8빠진 것 없는 문장 앞에 what 을 씀

    예

    I think what she is right.

    I think that she is right.

  2. ②three

문제 5

마지막 체크 8문제

  • We visited Namsan, (A) [ that / which ] has a tower.
  • (B) [ That / What ] I need is a cup of tea.
  • Dad helped me (C) [ fix / fixing ] my bike.
  1. ①that — That — fix
  2. ②that — What — fixing
  3. ③which — That — fixing

  5. ⑤which — What — fixing

문제 8

마지막 체크 8문제

우리말과 일치하도록 <조건>에 맞게 영작하시오.

(1) 엄마는 아빠만큼 많은 커피를 마신다. (my mom, drink, coffee, my dad)

→

(2) 벤치에 앉아 있는 남자는 우리 선생님이다. (the man, sit, on the bench, our teacher)

→

조건
  • 괄호 안의 말을 모두 쓸 것(형태 변화 가능)
  • (1)은 as ~ as, (2)는 분사를 사용할 것
  • 현재 시제로 쓸 것
정답(1) My mom drinks as much coffee as my dad.(2) The man sitting on the bench is our teacher.

셀 수 없으면 much, 하는 쪽은 -ing 입니다.
