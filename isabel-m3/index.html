함정

점수가 깎이는 곳 9가지

  1. 1the way 와 how 를 함께 씀

    예

    This is the way how she solved it.

    This is how she solved it.

  2. 2완전한 문장 앞에 which 를 씀

    예장소 + 주어 + 동사 → where

    This is the town which my father grew up.

    This is the town where my father grew up.

  3. 3성격을 나타내는 형용사 뒤에 for 를 씀

    예kind · nice · careless → of

    It was kind for you to help me.

    It was kind of you to help me.

함정

점수가 깎이는 곳 9가지

  1. 4enough 를 형용사 앞에 씀

    예

    It is enough light for me to carry.

    It is light enough for me to carry.

  2. 5뒤 절을 the + 비교급으로 시작하지 않음

    예

    The more I run, the faster I get.

  3. 6-er 비교급 앞에 more 를 또 붙임

    예

    The more we talked, the more happier we felt.

    The more we talked, the happier we felt.

함정

점수가 깎이는 곳 9가지

    예
