함정

점수가 깎이는 곳 6가지

  1. 1「생략하지 않고」 조건에서 관계대명사를 뺌

    예「목적격 관계대명사를 생략하지 않고 작성」

    The cap I bought is blue.

    The cap which I bought is blue.

  2. 2관계절 끝에 목적어를 또 씀

    예

    This is the song which I like it.

    This is the song which I like.

  3. 3want·ask·advise + 목적어 뒤에 to 를 뺌

    예

    Dad asked me wash the dishes.

    Dad asked me to wash the dishes.

함정

점수가 깎이는 곳 6가지

  1. 4「~하지 말라고」를 don't 로 씀

    예

    She advised us don't run here.

    She advised us not to run here.

  2. 5가주어 문장의 의미상 주어를 주격으로 씀

    예

    It is hard for she to sing high notes.

    It is hard for her to sing high notes.

  3. 6성격 형용사 뒤에 for 를 씀

    예

    It was brave for him to say sorry.

    It was brave of him to say sorry.
