함정

점수가 깎이는 곳 8가지

  1. 1명사를 꾸미는 곳에 동사를 그대로 씀

    예「책을 읽고 있는 여자」

    The woman read a book on the bench is my aunt.

    The woman reading a book on the bench is my aunt.

  2. 2as 와 as 사이에 비교급을 씀

    예

    My bag is as heavier as yours.

    My bag is as heavy as yours.

  3. 3분사구문 뒤에 콤마를 빠뜨림

    예

    Walking along the beach she found a shell.

    Walking along the beach, she found a shell.

함정

점수가 깎이는 곳 8가지

  1. 4접속사 as 절과 주절의 시제를 다르게 씀

    예「키가 커짐에 따라 ~ 작아졌다」

    As he grows taller, his clothes became too small.

    As he grew taller, his clothes became too small.

  2. 5먼저 일어난 일을 과거 시제로 씀

    예

    When I got home, my sister already went to bed.

    When I got home, my sister had already gone to bed.

함정

점수가 깎이는 곳 8가지

  1. 6선행사가 있는데 what 을 씀

    예

    This is the bag what I bought.

    This is the bag that I bought.

  2. 7성격 형용사 뒤 의미상 주어를 for 로 씀

    예

    It was kind for you to help me.

    It was kind of you to help me.

  3. 8가정법 과거의 주절에 can · will 을 씀

    예

    If I had a car, I can pick you up.

    If I had a car, I could pick you up.
