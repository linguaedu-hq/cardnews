  1. 4과거보다 먼저 일어난 일에 has + 과거분사를 씀

    예

    When I got to the station, the train has already left.

    When I got to the station, the train had already left.

  2. 5과거완료 수동태에서 been 을 빠뜨림

    예

    When we came home, all the cookies had eaten.

    When we came home, all the cookies had been eaten.

  3. 6선행사 뒤에 관계대명사 what 을 씀

    예

    Show me the thing what you made.

    Show me what you made.

함정

점수가 깎이는 곳 9가지

  1. 7분사구문을 과거분사로 잘못 시작함

    예

    Walked home, I saw a rainbow.

    Walking home, I saw a rainbow.

  2. 8접속사 as 의 뜻을 하나로만 읽음

    예

    ~함에 따라As we grow, we learn.

    ~ 때문에As it rained, we stayed in.

  3. 9each 뒤 동사의 수를 맞추지 않음

    예

    Each student have a locker.

    Each student has a locker.
