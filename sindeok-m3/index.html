함정

점수가 깎이는 곳 8가지

  1. 1당하는 쪽인데 -ing 로 꾸밈

    예

    The cake making by Dad was sweet.

    The cake made by Dad was sweet.

  2. 2동사를 꾸미는 as ~ as 에 형용사를 씀

    예

    Ben ran as quick as Leo.

    Ben ran as quickly as Leo.

함정

점수가 깎이는 곳 8가지

  1. 3기준이 과거인데 has + 과거분사를 씀

    예

    When I called him, he has already gone to bed.

    When I called him, he had already gone to bed.

  2. 4had 뒤에 과거형을 씀

    예

    I realized that I had forgot my umbrella.

    I realized that I had forgotten my umbrella.

  3. 5선행사 뒤에 what 을 씀

    예

    This is the song what I like the most.

    This is the song that I like the most.

함정

점수가 깎이는 곳 8가지

  1. 6하는 쪽인데 과거분사로 분사구문을 시작함

    예

    Seen the dog, the boy ran away.

    Seeing the dog, the boy ran away.

  2. 7not 을 분사 뒤에 둠

    예

    Feeling not well, Jin stayed home.

    Not feeling well, Jin stayed home.

  3. 8as 절 뒤에 so 를 또 씀

    예

    As it was late, so we took a taxi.

    As it was late, we took a taxi.

문제 1

마지막 체크 8문제

우리말에 맞게 영작하시오.

(1) 우리가 극장에 도착했을 때, 영화는 이미 시작했었다. (get to, the theater, start)

→ .

(2) 그는 전에 그 박물관에 가 본 적이 있다고 말했다. (say, visit, the museum, before)

→ .

조건
  • 과거완료를 사용할 것
  • 괄호 안의 말을 활용하되 필요하면 형태를 바꿀 것
  • 1개의 완전한 문장으로 쓸 것
정답(1) When we got to the theater, the movie had already started.(2) He said that he had visited the museum before.

도착한 때 · 말한 때보다 먼저 일어난 일 → had + 과거분사

마지막 체크 8문제

빈칸에 that 이 들어갈 수 없는 것을 고르시오.

  1. ①This is the bike ______ my uncle gave me.
  2. ②I believe ______ she will win the race.
  3. ③______ she told me was a surprise.
  4. ④All ______ I want is a glass of water.

문제 4

마지막 체크 8문제

다음 문장과 뜻이 같은 것을 고르시오.

Jisu is taller than Mina.

  2. ②Mina is not as tall as Jisu.

문제 8

마지막 체크 8문제

자연스러운 대화가 되도록 (A)~(C)를 순서대로 배열한 것을 고르시오.

A: Do you know how to make a paper plane?

(A) Then, fold the top corners to the center.

(B) Sure. First, fold a sheet of paper in half.

(C) Last, fold it once more and throw it.

  1. ①(A) - (B) - (C)
  2. ②(B) - (A) - (C)
  3. ③(B) - (C) - (A)
  4. ④(C) - (A) - (B)
  5. ⑤(C) - (B) - (A)
정답②

Sure. First → Then → Last 차례
