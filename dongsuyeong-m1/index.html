함정

점수가 깎이는 곳 8가지

  1. 1「~할 때」 when 부분에 will 을 씀

    예

    I'll text you when I finish dinner.

  2. 2지난 일인데 when 부분을 현재형으로 씀

    예「어제 내가 Jina 를 만났을 때」

    When I meet Jina, she smiled.

    When I met Jina, she smiled.

함정

점수가 깎이는 곳 8가지

  1. 3to 뒤에 -ing 를 씀

    예

    Mina wants to joining the club.

    Mina wants to join the club.

  2. 4감각동사 뒤에 -ly 부사를 씀

    예

    The pizza smells deliciously.

    The pizza smells delicious.

  3. 5명사 앞에 like 를 빠뜨림

    예

    This cookie looks a star.

    This cookie looks like a star.

함정

점수가 깎이는 곳 8가지

  1. 6more 와 -er 을 함께 씀

    예

    Tom is more taller than Sam.

    Tom is taller than Sam.

  2. 7enjoy 뒤에 to부정사를 씀

    예

    Dad enjoys to cook on weekends.

    Dad enjoys cooking on weekends.

  3. 8buy · make · cook 뒤 사람 앞에 to 를 씀

    예
