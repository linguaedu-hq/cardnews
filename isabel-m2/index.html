함정 2개
  • too heavy for me to lift it → it 삭제눌러서 펼쳐 보세요

  • enough tall → tall enough

    Leo is enough tall to reach the shelf.

    Leo is tall enough to reach the shelf.

    enough 는 형용사 뒤에 씁니다.

함정

점수가 깎이는 곳 8가지

  1. 1「목적격 관계대명사 포함」 조건에 생략함

    예「목적격 관계대명사를 반드시 포함할 것」

    The pen I lost was new.

    The pen which I lost was new.

  2. 2관계절 끝에 목적어를 또 씀

    예

    This is the song which I like it.

    This is the song which I like.

  3. 3used to 뒤에 -ing 를 씀

    예

    My dad used to playing tennis.

    My dad used to play tennis.

함정

점수가 깎이는 곳 8가지

  1. 4Did 뒤에 used to 를 그대로 씀

    예

    Did you used to live here?

    Did you use to live here?

  2. 5지각동사 + 목적어 뒤에 to부정사를 씀

    예

    We saw a bird to fly away.

    We saw a bird flying away.

함정

점수가 깎이는 곳 8가지

  1. 6so ~ that 의 that 뒤 시제를 맞추지 않음

    예

    It was so dark that I can't see.

    It was so dark that I couldn't see.

  2. 7「순서대로 사용할 것」 조건의 낱말 순서를 바꿈

    예「she / a letter / me 를 순서대로 사용」

    She gave me a letter.

    She gave a letter to me.

  3. 8걸어서 = on foot 을 by foot 으로 씀

    예

    I go to school by foot.

    I go to school on foot.
