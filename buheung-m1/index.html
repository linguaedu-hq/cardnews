함정

점수가 깎이는 곳 9가지

  1. 1enjoy 뒤에 to부정사를 씀

    예

    We enjoyed to swim in the sea.

    We enjoyed swimming in the sea.

  2. 2look forward to 뒤에 동사원형을 씀

    예

    I'm looking forward to meet you.

    I'm looking forward to meeting you.

  3. 3be going to 에서 be동사를 빠뜨림

    예

    My sister going to make a cake.

    My sister is going to make a cake.

함정

점수가 깎이는 곳 9가지

  1. 4비교급에 more 와 -er 를 함께 씀

    예

    This is more cheaper than mine.

    This is cheaper than mine.

  2. 5최상급을 most + 짧은 형용사로 씀

    예

    Jun is the most tall boy in my class.

    Jun is the tallest boy in my class.

  3. 6There is / are 를 뒤 명사의 수에 맞추지 않음

    예

    There is three cats in the yard.

    There are three cats in the yard.

함정

점수가 깎이는 곳 9가지

  1. 7must not 뒤에 to 를 넣음

    예

    You must not to run in the hallway.

    You must not run in the hallway.

  2. 8to부정사의 to 뒤 동사에 -s 를 붙임

    예

    She wants to becomes a pilot.

    She wants to become a pilot.

  3. 9「접속사 that 포함」 조건에서 that 을 빠뜨림

    예「접속사 that 을 반드시 포함할 것」

    I know that Jisu is kind.
