6과 · 문법 ①

to부정사 명사적 용법

주어: To + 동사원형 ~ (단수 취급)

To exercise regularly is not easy.

목적어: want · plan · decide + to + 동사원형

Ted decides to go camping this weekend.

보어: be동사 + to + 동사원형

My dream is to be a vet.

함정

점수가 깎이는 곳 8가지

  1. 1There 뒤 명사의 수를 보지 않고 be동사를 씀

    예

    There is two parks in my town.

    There are two parks in my town.

  2. 2more 와 -er 을 함께 씀

    예

    This box is more heavier than mine.

    This box is heavier than mine.

  3. 3조동사 뒤에 과거형을 씀

    예

    I can't found my key yesterday.

    I couldn't find my key yesterday.

함정

점수가 깎이는 곳 8가지

  1. 4plan · decide · agree 뒤에 -ing 를 씀

    예

    We plan visiting the zoo.

    We plan to visit the zoo.

  2. 5지시어 that 과 접속사 that 을 혼동함

    예

    지시어(저 ~)I like that bag.

    접속사I think that the bag is nice.

함정

점수가 깎이는 곳 8가지

  1. 6전치사 to 와 to부정사의 to 를 혼동함

    예

    전치사I go to the park.

    to부정사I want to go home.

  2. 7단어 수 조건을 세지 않음

    예「5단어로 쓸 것」

    She reads books.

    She reads books every day.

  3. 8주어 · 동사 없이 낱말로만 답함

    예What did Tom buy?

    A blue cap.

    He bought a blue cap.
