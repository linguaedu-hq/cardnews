함정

점수가 깎이는 곳 8가지

  1. 1가주어 문장에서 to 를 빠뜨림

    예

    It is fun join a club.

    It is fun to join a club.

  2. 2의미상 주어를 주격으로 씀

    예

    It is easy for they to ride bikes.

    It is easy for them to ride bikes.

  3. 3성격 형용사 뒤에 for 를 씀

    예

    It was brave for her to sing.

    It was brave of her to sing.

함정

점수가 깎이는 곳 8가지

  1. 4every 뒤에 복수 명사를 씀

    예

    Every players wears a red cap.

    Every player wears a red cap.

  2. 5each 주어 뒤에 복수 동사를 씀

    예

    Each team have five members.

    Each team has five members.

  3. 6지각동사 뒤에 to부정사를 씀

    예

    I watched them to flip in the air.

    I watched them flip in the air.

함정

점수가 깎이는 곳 8가지

  1. 7지각동사 목적어 뒤 동사에 -s 를 붙임

    예

    She saw Tom washes the dishes.

    She saw Tom wash the dishes.

  2. 8지각동사 목적어를 주격으로 씀

    예

    I saw he running to the bus stop.

    I saw him running to the bus stop.
