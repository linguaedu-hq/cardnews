함정

점수가 깎이는 곳 8가지

  1. 1관계절 동사를 선행사의 수에 맞추지 않음

    예

    I have a friend who live in Busan.

    I have a friend who lives in Busan.

  2. 2관계절 안에 주어를 다시 씀

    예

    I met a man who he teaches math.

    I met a man who teaches math.

  3. 3지각동사의 목적어 뒤에 to부정사를 씀

    예

    I heard the rain to fall.

    I heard the rain falling.

함정

점수가 깎이는 곳 8가지

  1. 4지각동사의 목적어를 주격으로 씀

    예

    We watched they dance on stage.

    We watched them dance on stage.

  2. 5수동태의 be동사를 주어의 수에 맞추지 않음

    예

    The cookies was made by my dad.

    The cookies were made by my dad.

  3. 6동작을 받는 주어인데 수동태로 쓰지 않음

    예

    English speaks in Canada.

    English is spoken in Canada.

함정

점수가 깎이는 곳 8가지

  1. 7that 앞 형용사에 so 대신 very 를 씀

    예

    The test was very hard that I couldn't finish it.

    The test was so hard that I couldn't finish it.

  2. 8「다른 단어를 추가하지 말 것」 조건에 낱말을 더 씀

    예▶ (be / so / she / that / sad / cry / she)

    She was so sad that she cried a lot.

    She was so sad that she cried.

문제 6

마지막 체크 8문제

두 문장을 한 문장으로 바꿔 쓰시오.

(1) Jin has a sister. She sings well.

→ .

(2) I like the cat. It has blue eyes.

→ .

조건
  • (1)은 who, (2)는 which 를 사용할 것
정답(1) Jin has a sister who sings well.(2) I like the cat which has blue eyes.

She · It 을 who · which 로 바꿔 붙입니다.

→ .

(2) My grandfather planted this tree.
